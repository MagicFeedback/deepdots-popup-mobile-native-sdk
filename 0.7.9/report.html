Release notes 0.7.9

Fixed

  • iOS: a survey taller than its box can be scrolled again. The survey's WebView sat inside the popup's scrollable middle with the default (cooperative) touch handling: the middle took every vertical drag, could not move because its content already fitted, and the WebView never scrolled. On a small iPhone (SE) the 0–10 scale, shown as a vertical list there, could not be scrolled past 3, so 4–10 could not be chosen. The survey now gets its touches directly and scrolls its own content; its content stays available to VoiceOver. The console bridge of the iOS WebView also drops optional chaining (iOS 13.0–13.3).

  • iOS: the keyboard no longer covers the popup's Back and Send. The SDK relied on the Compose view controller to move the focused field above the keyboard, but the survey's text field lives in a WKWebView, which Compose does not see as focused: nothing moved, and on a free-text page (focused on its own after Next) the keyboard covered the footer until it was dismissed. Seen on every iPhone in the device matrix (iPhone SE to iPhone 17). The popup now keeps itself above the keyboard with the IME inset, as on Android, and the view controller no longer shifts the view on focus, so it is not moved twice.

  • The close button (X) stays visible when the survey does not fit. With a tall survey page, the whole card scrolled, and after a "required" message it scrolled to the bottom on its own: the title and the X went out of view, so the popup could not be closed without scrolling back up. The title row and the buttons now stay fixed and only the content in between scrolls. Found by the end-to-end tests on iOS.

  • The popup's Send, Back, close and Start work on older WebViews. The native buttons ran window.DeepdotsActions?.send() in the survey; a WebView older than Chrome 80 (Android 8–10 phones whose WebView was never updated) and iOS 13.0–13.3 reject optional chaining with a SyntaxError, so the button did nothing and pages that need Send never moved on (the rating page worked because it advances by itself). The calls are now plain ES5, and a test fails if the survey's scripts use ?. or ?? again. Found by the local device matrix on Android 8.

  • Android 8–10: the keyboard no longer covers the popup's Back and Send. The popup is mounted on the window's decor view, outside the content the Activity resizes, and before Android 11 the keyboard was not reported to it as an inset, so imePadding() was 0 and the footer stayed under the keyboard. On those versions the popup now measures the keyboard from the window's visible frame and moves above it; Android 11+ keeps using the IME inset.

  • iOS: a popup at the top no longer goes under the status bar. With a top position and a tall survey, the card was laid out from the very top of the screen: the title sat on the clock and the close button (X) under the battery icon, where it could not be tapped. The card now stays inside the safe area (status bar, Dynamic Island / notch, home indicator); the dimmed backdrop still covers the whole screen. Found by the new end-to-end tests.
