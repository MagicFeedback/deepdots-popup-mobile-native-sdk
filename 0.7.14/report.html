Release notes 0.7.14

Survey SDK supported: 2.2.38–2.2.44 (survey frontend 3.4.1–3.6.0); bundled: 2.2.44.

Added

  • The progress bar can count answered questions (DD-210). With style.progressMode: "completed" the bar starts at 0 %, shows "2 of 5 answered" on question 3, does not move while an AI follow-up is open, and shows 100 % ("5 of 5 answered") on the thank-you screen. Missing or "current" keeps today's bar. Same rules as the web popup and the survey link.

Fixed

  • The question stays in view with the keyboard open on small phones. With the survey fitted to the card (see below), centring the focused text field pushed the question text out of view on a 360x640 screen. The survey now scrolls the question block (question text and the first line of the field) to the top, and only the field when even that does not fit.
  • The end of the survey is no longer hidden when the popup has a logo. The survey area took 72 % of the card without counting the logo, progress bar, message or error banner. With a logo the middle of the card overflowed and scrolled on its own, but a finger on the survey only scrolls the WebView, so the bottom of the survey stayed under the footer: the last number of a 0-10 list, the last option of a question, and on iPhone the text field with the keyboard open (Lemvigh-Müller, Galaxy S21/S25 and iPhone 17). The survey now gets the height that is left in the card and scrolls inside. Android and iOS.
  • The logo folds away while the keyboard is open. On text questions the card shrinks with the keyboard and the logo kept its space, so the question text went out of view. The logo comes back when the keyboard closes. Android and iOS.
  • iPads are reported as iOS. On iPad UIDevice.systemName is "iPadOS", so deepdots_sdk said android@<version> and analytics batches had platform: android. Both now say ios.

Tests

  • Key popup checks on real devices (DD-273). Open popup, X / Send / Back with a finger-like tap, keyboard over the text question and the survey filling its slot on a tablet, as Android instrumented tests with the real WebView (shared/src/androidInstrumentedTest) and as XCUITest on NORDLY (test-apps/ios/NordlyDeviceChecks). New workflow testlab.yml runs them in Firebase Test Lab on a fixed device list (testlab/devices.json) only on release PRs and by hand, with a 10 min timeout, no retries and the billed minutes in the job summary. No change to the SDK itself: the build only gains the instrumented test set-up.
