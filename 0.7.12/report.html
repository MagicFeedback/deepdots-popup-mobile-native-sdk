Release notes 0.7.12

Survey SDK supported: 2.2.38–2.2.39 (survey frontend 3.4.1–3.6.0); bundled: 2.2.39. Compatibility table: docs.deepdots.com/popup-native/reference/survey-compatibility/.

Added

  • Catalan (ca) popup texts. Buttons, progress, aria-labels and error notices in Catalan; ca/ca-ES resolve to it. Same strings as the web SDK. Catalan is not an official EU language; asked for in the v2.2.29 review.

Fixed

  • The popup's X, Send, Back and Start respond to a real finger again. Since 0.7.2 the dimmed backdrop consumed every touch so it would not reach the host app. The card with the buttons is a child of that backdrop, and when a parent consumes the small movements a finger always makes between pressing and lifting, the button cancels the click. On real phones the native buttons often did nothing (the survey itself, in the WebView, still answered), so the popup could not be closed or moved past a page that needs Send. Automated taps have no movement, which is why the test suites did not see it. The backdrop now receives the touches without consuming them: they still stop at the popup (the host app does not get them) and the buttons work. Both Android and iOS. Reported by Lemvigh-Müller on Galaxy S25 (Android 16 and 17).
  • Tablets: the survey uses the whole height the popup gives it. The survey's WebView had a fixed height of 520dp. On a tablet the popup has more room than that, so the survey stopped at 520dp, a 0-10 scale shown as a list was cut in the middle (it had to be scrolled inside) and a band of white space stayed between it and Send. The WebView now fills its area, up to the popup's maximum height as before. Phones were not affected (their area is below 520dp). Both Android and iOS. Reported by Lemvigh-Müller on a Samsung tablet.
  • The text box stays in view when the keyboard opens. With the keyboard open the popup is lifted above it and its survey area gets shorter; a text question under a long title (small phones, large font sizes) was left below the visible part, so the user typed without seeing the text. The survey now scrolls the focused field into view when its area shrinks or a field gets focus. Both Android and iOS.
